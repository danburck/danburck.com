01The read

The revenue says it's working. Your Tuesday says otherwise.

You're not short on ambition. You're short on clarity about where your energy actually goes.

So you chase every opportunity, because none of them can be ruled out. You take on responsibilities that were never yours to solve. You run on willpower, and willpower is a loan against next week. And somewhere in there you became the bottleneck in your own company.

More hours won't buy this back. You already tried that. The hours are not the constraint.

Shift your first principle from time to energy, and the calendar reorganizes itself.

02What this isn't

Not a course. Not therapy. Not another productivity system.

Not a course

You don't have a knowledge problem. You've read the books, you know about deep work, you own three planners. Each module here takes twenty minutes. The work is in the exercise afterwards, and I'm on the call while you do it.

Not therapy

We're not excavating your childhood for its own sake. We find the one loop that is running your business this quarter, name it out loud, and rewire it this week. Business outcomes, not insight for its own sake.

Not a productivity system

Time management assumes the operator is at full charge. You're not. Prioritisation done at low energy is aiming blindfolded. Fix the operator first and the prioritising gets easy.

What it actually is

Ten modules, weekly 1:1 coaching, and a personal focus system built around your real role in the business. You own everything we build. It runs after we stop working together.

03The system

Track. Rewire. Deploy.

Three phases, in order. Each one is useless without the one before it.

I

Track

Detect the leaks. You cannot beat what you don't know exists. We collect real data on your energy states, then name the patterns and loops that are quietly setting your ceiling.

II

Rewire

Break the autopilot. The belief underneath the loop gets made visible, neutralised and replaced, then you rebuild self-trust through evidence rather than affirmations.

III

Deploy

Build the focus system. Five rituals that shape your environment, activate your state, filter for leverage, hand off what isn't yours, and keep you honest. Energy invested where it compounds.

10Questions

Before you book.

Is the call actually free, or is it a sales call in disguise?+

It's free and it's real coaching. You get the energy map, the named leak and one ritual to run. I'll mention the program once, at the end, and only if I think it fits. If you never book anything after, the thirty minutes still did their job.

How is this different from productivity coaching?+

Productivity coaching optimises the schedule of an operator running at half charge. We work on the charge. Track your energy states first, rewire what's draining them, then build the focus system on top. The prioritising becomes obvious once the input is clean.

I'm overwhelmed right now. Isn't twelve weeks of homework the last thing I need?+

Every exercise is designed to be doable on a low-energy day. That's a rule, not a courtesy. If a practice feels daunting we shrink it until it almost feels too small to count. We call that a Minimum Viable Promise, and it's the unit the whole focus system is built from. That's where we start, and that's how self-trust actually gets rebuilt.

Do I need to be doing seven figures?+

No. What matters is that you're carrying real responsibility and you've hit a ceiling that more effort isn't moving. That happens at very different revenue levels. We'll know within ten minutes of the call.

What actually happens over the twelve weeks?+

Weekly 1:1 sessions, the ten modules with an exercise each, personalised visualisation and breathwork practices, and me reachable between sessions when something breaks. You finish with a personal focus system you can rely on long after we stop working together.

What does it cost?+

We cover it on the call, once I know what you actually need. Working with me one-to-one is a serious commitment of money and attention, and I'd rather quote you honestly than post a number that fits nobody.
